## returns_model.json

Summary statistics (means, covariances and one tail parameter of log real returns on shares, housing and bills)
computed from the Jordà-Schularick-Taylor Macrohistory Database (R6), https://www.macrohistory.net/database/, consulted
2026-10-13. The database itself (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) is not redistributed.
Òscar Jordà, Moritz Schularick, and Alan M. Taylor (2017). "Macrofinancial History and the New Business Cycle Facts."
NBER Macroeconomics Annual 2016, vol. 31. Òscar Jordà, Katharina Knoll, Dmitry Kuvshinov, Moritz Schularick, and
Alan M. Taylor (2019). "The Rate of Return on Everything, 1870–2015." Quarterly Journal of Economics 134(3): 1225–1298.
